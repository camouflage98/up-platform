The Process

Three acts.
One story.

UP is not a job board. It's a place where wisdom finds ambition, and where an unfinished life's work becomes someone else's greatest beginning.

  • i.

    Post Your Project

    Elderly creators describe their unfinished work — what they built, why it matters, and what still needs doing. No project is too personal or too grand.

  • ii.

    Find Your Successor

    Young people browse, discover, and apply. We help match by skill, passion, and geography — prioritising depth of connection over speed of placement.

  • iii.

    Pass the Torch

    Creator and successor meet — virtually or in person. Knowledge is transferred. The project lives on. The story finds its next chapter.

RN
Robert Nakamura, 81
Water Purification Device
Vancouver, Canada

I spent forty years building a prototype for a water-purification device. My hands aren't steady enough anymore. When I posted on UP, a young engineer from Kenya wrote to me. Now she has my blueprints. I cry every time she sends an update.

LF
Lucia Ferreira, 76
Family Recipe Cookbook
Porto, Portugal

My grandmother's recipes were locked inside me. I had started a cookbook but never finished — who would want to read it? UP gave me the courage. A food writer in Bologna reached out. My nonna's ragù will finally be in print. I feel such peace.

JO
James Okoye, 79
Elementary Mathematics Curriculum
Lagos, Nigeria

I was a teacher for fifty years. I wrote a mathematics curriculum I know could change how children learn. Retired, I had no way to share it. Through UP, a school in rural India has started piloting it. This is the best thing that has happened to me in decades.
