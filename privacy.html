Privacy Policy

Plain language. No surprises. We take your data seriously.

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

When you join our waitlist, we collect your email address. That's it. We do not collect your name, phone number, location, or any other personal information unless you choose to provide it.

If you submit a project through our Tally form, we collect the information you choose to share about your project (such as a description, your first name, and how we can contact you).

How we use it

  • To send you a single notification when UP launches
  • To reach out about your submitted project
  • We will never sell your email address or share it with third parties for marketing

Email and marketing

We use Brevo to manage our email list. Your email is stored on their platform. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send you.

We do not send promotional emails, newsletters, or any communication beyond what you signed up for.

Project submissions

Project submissions are collected via Tally.so and stored on their platform. We use this information solely to understand what projects people want to share and to contact submitters when the platform is ready.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics trackers, or any third-party tracking scripts. We do not know who visits this page or how many times.

Your rights

You can request that we delete your email address at any time by writing to us at hello@up-platform.org. We will remove it within 7 days.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how we handle your data, we will update this page and note the date. We won't make changes that harm your privacy without notice.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us directly at hello@up-platform.org. We read every message.
